iHYPE

Boilerplate

iHYPE is a not-for-profit live music platform built on a single founding constraint: the company takes nothing from ticket sales. Every ticket splits 70% to the artist, 20% to the venue, and 10% to a promoter pool shared by whoever helped sell it — locked in iHYPE's charter and unchangeable without dissolving and re-incorporating the company. iHYPE is funded entirely by music-related advertising, the same way terrestrial radio has always worked. Founded in Portland, Maine in January 2026, iHYPE is run by two people and AI automation.
