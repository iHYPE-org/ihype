§ 01There is one ground

Light, dark and console were collapsed into a single ground. There is no theme switcher, no prefers-color-scheme, no [data-theme] block. A template must not offer an appearance choice.

--bg#f0dfb8the board
--ink#1c1408body copy — 13.84:1

html.high-contrast still exists and is not a theme — it is an accessibility mode with its own black ground. Do not design for it; do not remove it.

§ 02Materials

The look is not “a warm palette”. It is three materials used for three kinds of object. Getting this wrong is what makes a page read as a beige website instead of a receiver.

Walnut is dark everywhere — it carries its own ink

This is the most important rule here, and it has already caused one shipped bug: the player dock painted dark ink on a near-black bar for weeks.

Ink on the cabinet

--ink-on-walnut-2 — secondary copy, 7.61:1 against the lightest stop.

--ink-on-walnut-3 · 5.51:1 · the floor for a word
--ink-on-walnut#f6ecd910.87:1 on --walnut
--ink-on-walnut-2#d8c6a67.61:1 — secondary
--ink-on-walnut-3#bda8825.51:1 — floor for a word

Ratios are against --walnut, the lightest stop. Copy can land anywhere on a gradient, so the worst case is the only one worth quoting. Never put --ink, --ink-2 or --ink-3 on walnut — there is a lint rule that fails the build on it. Hairlines use --rule-on-walnut; the page's --line vanishes into timber.

§ 03The accent is a fill, never a word

--accent is #ff5029. On the board it measures 2.48:1 — it fails AA as copy and fails even the 3:1 large-text bar.

Correct label 5.58:1 · pass White label 3.27:1 · fails

The accent as a word uses --accent-text #923319 — 5.89:1. --ink-on-accent is dark ink, not white. This surprises people; it is measured.

The same split exists for --warning-text, --danger-text and --success-text. A fill token and a copy token are different tokens even when the hex matches.

§ 04The tuner — drag it

It replaces every horizontal tab strip. A strip divides one row by the number of tabs, so each tab added shrinks every label; the dial spends the same row on one destination at 26px and does not shrink when a section is added.

Discover

Drag the face, use the wheel, or press the brass keys. The scale is two repeating gradients driven by the drag — infinite in both directions, no cloned strip, no seam, stations wrap. In the app it is a role="tablist" with roving tabindex, never a slider: a slider announces a number where a member needs a destination.

The recessed plate

Artwork sits in a machined bezel — deep brass, bright brass, deep again, with an inner top shadow so it reads as cut into the faceplate. Radius 2px: a bezel is turned metal, and a large round-rect reads as a phone app icon.

§ 05Type

Three faces, three jobs — and this page is set in them.

RoleFaceRule
Page displayBricolage Grotesque800 weight, tight tracking
Section headingInstrument Serifh2, via --f-s
ProseWork Sansbody
Eyebrow / dataJetBrains Monotracked, uppercase
  1. 15px is the content floor. Enforced — the build fails below it.
  2. 11px is the eyebrow floor, and only for tracked mono: monospace family and letter-spacing ≥ 0.14em. Metadata only — a form label, an error message or a status readout is content, not an eyebrow.
  3. Sizes in rem, never px — the root size carries the reader's Text size setting and iOS Dynamic Type, and px cannot follow it.

§ 06Rules a template must not break

  1. No emoji. Unicode glyphs (▶ ❚❚ ♥ ✕ ✓ ★ ⬟ ♪) are the vocabulary.
  2. There is no DJ role. Deleted from the product 2026-08-06.
  3. Promoter is not an account type. It is a 10% payout share; --role-promoter colours that slice and nothing else.
  4. No white on the accent fill. Use --ink-on-accent.
  5. No appearance or theme switcher. One ground.
  6. 70 / 20 / 10 / 0% — artist / venue / promoters / iHYPE. Never restate it differently.
  7. Audio only. No video anywhere.
  8. admin@ihype.org and ihype.org are the only contact and domain.

§ 08What the code does not have

Stated so a template does not assume a control nobody has built.

  1. Transport knob with drag gestures — tap play/pause, drag for next/prev, drag up for the full player. Not built.
  2. The map is a real interactive map, toned to an aged chart — not the prototype's drawn coastline. Real venues sit at real coordinates.
  3. Marketing, legal and admin routes carry the palette and type but not the cabinet. Only the app shell is furniture.
