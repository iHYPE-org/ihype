# Adherence rules — iHYPE design system

Rules a reviewer (or a linter) can check mechanically. Written for Claude Code
working in `iHYPE-org/ihype`. `_adherence.oxlintrc.json` is generated by the
design-system compiler; this file is the human-readable companion and covers the
things a config cannot express.

## Product facts that are not style choices

1. **Payments are Stripe only.** Every ticket purchase carries Stripe's fee —
   2.9% + $0.30, or 3.5% + $0.30 for Amex. Stripe charges it, not iHYPE.
2. **iHYPE's own fee is $0** and the 70/20/10 charter split is unaffected. Any UI
   or copy showing fees must keep "iHYPE fees ($0, locked in charter)" and
   "payment processing (Stripe, separate)" as two separate numbers. Never merge
   them; never imply iHYPE takes a processing cut.
3. **Promoter is not an account role.** No signup, no verification, no account
   type. Any Fan, Artist or Venue promotes by sharing a HYPE link. Never add
   "Promoter" to a role picker. Promoter dashboards are views of a fan's own
   activity.
4. **There is no DJ role.** Deleted 2026-08-06. `--role-dj` is stale wherever it
   appears.
5. The four account types are **Fan · Artist · Venue · Advertiser**.

## Shell

6. **No header and no tab bar** on `/app/*`. The only persistent chrome is the
   logo trigger, the player pill and the nav hint, all bottom-left/bottom-right.
7. **The map is the base layer and stays mounted.** It belongs in the `/app`
   layout, not a page — a page re-mounts it and loses pan and zoom.
8. **Module, tab and panel are routes, not state.** `/app/map`,
   `/app/music/<tab>`, `/app/me/<panel>`. Only `navOpen`, `navSection`, `sheet`,
   `playing` and `hyped` are ephemeral.
9. **Only MUSIC has a submenu.** MAP and ME navigate directly. ME is flat, with
   Settings · Info · Legal · Accessibility as in-page rows.
10. **The arc has exactly five level-2 slots.** A module with more items than
    that silently loses the overflow — assert it, don't trust it.
11. **A closed arc item must not be tabbable.** `pointer-events: none` hides it
    from the mouse but not the keyboard; set `tabIndex={-1}` and `aria-hidden`.
12. **The transform and delay are computed per index and passed inline.** Do not
    route them through a custom property — an undefined custom property
    invalidates the whole declaration silently and every item animates at once.
13. **One scroll container: the module pane.** `html`/`body` stay locked.
14. **The pin sheet belongs to the map.** Leaving MAP must clear it.
15. **Never render a control that is guaranteed to fail.** The player's heart is
    absent when the artist has no linked profile, is not discoverable, or is the
    viewer; play/pause is absent when the pill shows a listen record with no URL.
16. **Never name a prop after a reserved HTML attribute.** `open` and `hidden`
    are boolean attributes and get dropped before the component sees them, so the
    shell components take `expanded` and `dimmed` instead. Multi-word props must
    be kebab-case at the call site — HTML lowercases attribute names, so
    `canHype` arrives as `canhype` and silently never binds. This cost a
    non-opening arc nav once already.

## Geography

17. **Only venues have addresses.** A venue is a place: it carries a real street
    address and its pin lands on the door. Artists and promoters carry a **city
    of origin** and nothing finer — the product never collects an address for
    them, so no map, profile or share card may imply one. City-level pins are
    visibly distinct from venue pins (dashed, softer, no address in the sheet).
18. **Never freehand geography.** Real tiles or real geometry. Street-level
    interactive maps use Leaflet; the basemap is CARTO's dark raster build of
    OpenStreetMap data, because `tile.openstreetmap.org` is a volunteer service
    that 403s embedded use. The "© OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO"
    attribution is a license requirement, not a caption — never remove it.
19. **A map lives in a plain HTML file, never inside a Design Component.** A DC
    confines scripts to `<helmet>`, whose mount timing races the map container.
    The shell embeds `map.html` and receives pin selections by message — the same
    seam `MmmMap` → `MmmShell` uses.

## Player

20. **The player docks to the trigger.** Immediately right of the logo, sharing
    its baseline, pinned to the bottom of the frame. `left` = trigger inset +
    trigger width + gap; never hardcode it in two places.
21. **Opening the nav dims everything, the player included.** It fades and drops
    rather than unmounting, so the transition plays out both ways, and it is made
    non-interactive while dimmed. Tapping the logo again, or anywhere outside the
    fan, restores it.
22. **HYPE and favourite are two controls, not one.** HYPE spends from your
    balance and moves the artist up the local chart; the heart only saves the
    track to your library. Never collapse them — the mechanic the product is
    named after disappears into a bookmark. The HYPE control carries the bolt
    from the mark, drawn as SVG, because the obvious glyph for it is an emoji.
23. **No reveal may be produced by an animation or a transition — none.**
    `document.timeline.currentTime` does not advance in every context this system
    runs in (embedded previews, some export pipelines). A `both`-filled keyframe
    or an unadvanced transition then holds its FROM value permanently, so anything
    fading in from `opacity: 0` stays at zero forever while its inline style and
    its React props both read "visible". Author every element at its final value
    and make appearance a state change. This cost four rounds: first a
    transition, then "gate it on one rAF" (a single frame can be delivered without
    the timeline ticking), then keyframes (frozen identically). An animation is
    only safe if its FROM state is already the readable one and it never fills —
    `ih-marquee` qualifies; a fade-in never does.
24. **Verify reveal states with `getComputedStyle`, never with props or a
    screenshot.** Props and the style attribute read "open" in exactly the case
    above, and `html-to-image` re-renders the DOM so a screenshot ignores frozen
    animation state entirely. Check `getComputedStyle(el).opacity === "1"`.
25. **Cache-bust every asset a page pulls from the design system, not just the
    bundle.** `_ds_bundle.js` and the token CSS are rewritten in place at the same
    URLs, so a plain `<link>`/`<script src>` serves whatever the browser already
    has — silently rendering the previous generation of the components AND the
    tokens. `ds-base.js` appends a version query to all of them.
26. **The track line scrolls only when it overflows.** A marquee on a short title
    is motion for its own sake, and `prefers-reduced-motion` disables it outright.
27. **Queue and history are one list, split at the current index.** "Up next" is
    what follows, "played" is what precedes, greyed and below a rule. Two arrays
    drift apart.
28. **The player owns no audio element.** Everything it shows is passed in and
    every control is a callback — which is what lets the same component sit over
    `MediaPlayerProvider` in the product and over prototype state in the template.

## Visual

29. **No emoji, anywhere.** Expressiveness comes from typographic contrast and
    color. Unicode glyphs (`▶ ❚❚ ♥ ♡ ✕ ◂ ›`) are fine; `🔥`/`🎤`/`🏛` are not.
30. **Use tokens, not hexes.** `var(--accent)`, not `#ff5029`. A literal hex in
    new code is a bug unless it is a documented fallback (`var(--x,#hex)`).
31. **Display type needs two settings, not one.** Bricolage Grotesque is
    variable: set `opsz` to the pixel size *and* `letter-spacing: -.035em` at
    headline sizes.
32. **Role fills stay vivid; role *copy* uses the `-text` variants.**
    `--accent-text`, `--role-venue-text`, `--warning-text` etc. carry the light
    theme. Never darken a brand fill to fix contrast — that splits a composed
    alpha tint from the solid fill beside it.
33. **AA is a floor, not a goal.** `--ink-3` is the tightest ink in the system;
    re-measure it against any new surface before shipping the surface.
34. **Dark is canonical, light is real.** The light theme is axe-verified and
    shipped. Anything new must work in both.
35. **New radii are `--radius-card` (18px) and pill.** `--radius-sm/md/lg` exist
    for continuity with the codebase; do not renumber them.

## Music surfaces

36. **Discover is the seed module.** Its deck is proximity seeds and
    hype-history recommendations shuffled together, and every card states which
    one put it there — a recommendation you cannot account for is
    indistinguishable from an ad. Recommended is the same recommendation items on
    their own, drawn from one list so the two surfaces never disagree about what
    a recommendation is.
37. **Universal search is persistent on MUSIC, and is not a tab.** It scopes
    across artists, tracks, venues, cities and playlists. MAP has its own search;
    ME has none.

## Copy

38. Second person, direct. "Your data · this week", never "User data".
39. Eyebrows and metadata are all-caps mono, tracked out. Headlines are display,
    tight, and say one thing.
40. State the mechanism, not the marketing. "Proximity did", not "our smart
    algorithm".
