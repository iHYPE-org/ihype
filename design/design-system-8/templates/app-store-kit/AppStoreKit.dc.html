Description
iHYPE is live music built on one rule: the platform takes nothing from ticket sales.

Every ticket splits 70% to the artist, 20% to the venue, 10% to a promoter pool shared by everyone who helped spread the word — locked in our founding charter. No service fees, no processing markup beyond the unavoidable cost of accepting a card.

Listen to the radio, hype the artists you believe in before anyone else has heard of them, and buy tickets at face value — direct.

iHYPE is funded by music-only advertising, the same way terrestrial radio always has been. No data sales. No streaming cuts. No middlemen.

App Store & Play Console

Review notes

Paste into App Review Information and Play Console → App content. The demo account must stay seeded with one upcoming show and one purchased ticket.

Read this first — payments

Tickets are admission to real, in-person events — physical goods and services consumed outside the app. Under App Store Review Guideline 3.1.3(e) and Play's payments policy, these are not digital content, so In-App Purchase does not apply and Stripe is the correct processor. iHYPE takes $0 from the ticket price; the only amount added at checkout is Stripe's processing fee (2.9% + $0.30, or 3.5% + $0.30 for Amex), which is charged by Stripe and disclosed as a separate line before payment. There is no digital-only content, subscription, or unlockable feature anywhere in the app.
