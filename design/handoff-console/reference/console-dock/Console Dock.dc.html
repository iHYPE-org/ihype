DOCK HEIGHT 74 + 8·2 + 3 = 93PX · CONSTANT ON EVERY PLATFORM

The dock is the same 93px on mobile, desktop, iOS and Android — it is derived from the knob, and the knob does not change size. Safe-area insets pad beneath it rather than growing it, so the content area above is the only thing that flexes and every platform gets the same layout.

THE KNOB REPORTS THE MODULE
MAP + Venues

The dock states where you are as well as where you can go. "MAP + Albums" reads as a profile opened from the map — which is why the dial tunes the sections of whatever you are looking at, not a fixed list.
